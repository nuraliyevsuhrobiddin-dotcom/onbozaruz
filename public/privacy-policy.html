Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how OBOX (OnBozar Marketplace), accessible at https://onbozar.uz, collects, uses, stores, and safeguards personal information of our users in full compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy, Supabase security standards, and applicable privacy regulations.

Effective Date: August 26, 2026
Official Website: https://onbozar.uz
Contact: support@onbozar.uz

1. Overview & Scope

OBOX (OnBozar) is an online B2B/B2C marketplace platform operating in Uzbekistan, dedicated to connecting producers, wholesale distributors, retail stores, and consumers. We value your privacy and are committed to maintaining the highest level of security and transparency regarding your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to operate, improve, and secure our marketplace platform:

  • Account Information: Name, business/store name, email address, phone number, location, and optional profile pictures.
  • Listings & Transactions: Product titles, descriptions, photos, videos, pricing, and order details.
  • Technical Information: IP address, device model, browser type, operating system, and essential local storage tokens for active authentication sessions.

3. Google OAuth & Google User Data Policy

OBOX provides users with an optional, secure one-click sign-in mechanism via Google OAuth 2.0.

Information Provided by Google:

When you sign in using Google, Google requests your permission to share basic profile information with OBOX:

  • Your Full Name: Used to display your name on your account profile and marketplace listings.
  • Your Verified Email Address: Used as your unique account identifier and for transactional notifications.
  • Your Profile Picture URL: Used optionally as your default account avatar.

Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use Disclosure:

OBOX's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We only request basic identity scopes: openid, email, and profile.
  • We do NOT request access to Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, Contacts, or sensitive scopes.
  • We NEVER sell, trade, or transfer Google user data to advertising platforms or data brokers.
  • Google user data is used solely to authenticate your identity and provide core marketplace functionality.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To create and manage your marketplace user account and seller profile.
  • To enable communication and order fulfillment between buyers and sellers.
  • To prevent fraudulent behavior, spam, unauthorized access, and protect platform security.
  • To send essential transactional notifications (e.g. order updates and email verifications).

5. Data Storage, Security & Supabase Architecture

Your data is stored securely using Supabase with PostgreSQL database architecture protected by Row Level Security (RLS) policies.

  • All network transmissions are encrypted with modern TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Passwords (if using email/password authentication) are cryptographically hashed; we never store plain-text passwords.
  • Database access is strictly isolated by user ID via Row-Level Security.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

OBOX uses essential cookies and browser localStorage tokens solely to maintain your authentication state and cache data for offline-first performance. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have the complete right to request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time:

  • In-App: Navigate to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  • By Email: Send a deletion request to support@onbozar.uz from your registered email address.

Upon receiving a deletion request, your data will be permanently purged from active databases within 30 days.

8. User Privacy Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, restrict, or delete your personal data. You can also revoke OBOX's access to your Google account at any time through Google Security Permissions.
